Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

The short version: we collect only what the game needs to run: your email to sign you in, the name you pick for the boards, and your game results. Your name and results are public on the leaderboards. Your email is never shown to other players. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

This policy explains what information Five a Day ("we," "us") collects when you use the Five a Day website (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

If you play as a guest without signing in, your results are saved only in your own browser (using local storage) and are not sent to us. When you later sign in, those saved results move to your account.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save your progress and show scores, ranks, leagues and challenges.
  • To keep the game fair: checking scores, filtering out bots and cheating, and moderating names and player-written questions.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To keep the Service working and secure, and to fix problems.

We don't send marketing emails. The only emails you'll get from us are the sign-in links you ask for, and replies if you contact us.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and save guest results on your device. We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings; doing so signs you out and removes any guest results that haven't been moved to an account.

4. Services we rely on

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use a few outside services to run the site, and they handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and the game database.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • An email delivery service: sends sign-in links.
  • Google Fonts: loads the site's fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the safety or rights of players or others, or as part of a sale or transfer of the Service (in which case this policy would still apply to your information).

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account information and game results for as long as your account exists. Leaderboards and past seasons are part of the game's history, so results stay until you ask us to delete your account. Server logs kept by our hosting providers are deleted on their own schedules.

6. Your choices

  • Change your name at any time from your account menu on the site.
  • Get a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account by emailing us at nathanjlarson06@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We'll delete your account, display name and game results within 30 days, except where we need to keep something to comply with the law.
  • Play without an account. Guest play keeps your results on your own device.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. Email us and we'll help.

7. Children

Five a Day is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

Sign-in uses one-time email links, so we never store a password for you. Database rules limit each player to changing only their own records. No system is perfectly secure, though, so we can't guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission.

9. Where information is stored

Five a Day is run from the United States. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll post a notice on the site. Using the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
