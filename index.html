        Fair play

        • Answers aren't in the page. Questions ship with scrambled answer checks, so viewing the source doesn't reveal them.
        • Every score is re-checked. Boards recompute each player's points from their saved picks and timing. Edited totals don't count.
        • One shot per round. Your attempt is locked the moment you start. Reloading doesn't reset it, and each answer saves as you go.
        • No tab switching. On a computer, leaving the tab mid-question forfeits that question, which stops quick searches. On a phone, the clock keeps running while you're away.
        • Bot filter. Correct answers faster than a human can read get flagged and left off the boards.
        • Your records are yours. Each player's rounds, leagues and challenges are stored where only they can write. Streaks, badges and challenge results are re-checked from saved rounds.
        • Moderation. The game's owner can hide a player from every board and reviews every player-written question.
